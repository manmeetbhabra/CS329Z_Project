Thacker Pass Project Reclamation Cost Estimate

Per 43 CFR 3809 and NAC 519A.365, operators are required to provide the BLM and NDEP with a Reclamation Cost Estimate.

The Reclamation Cost Estimate will be prepared at a later date when the Plan review and approval process has progressed to the point where the BLM, NDEP, and LNC (the operator) can anticipate what the approved Plan might look like. Per 43 CFR 3809.401(d), the operator will submit the Reclamation Cost Estimate during the appropriate time during the Plan review and approval process.
