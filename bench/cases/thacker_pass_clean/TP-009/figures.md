# Figures – Plan of Operations and Reclamation Plan

The Plan of Operations includes 19 figures, submitted as separate scanned drawing sheets. Their
text is not reproduced in this document set. Each entry gives the figure's title (from the Plan's
List of Figures), its source file, and a short description of what it shows, based on the title
and on how the Plan's text refers to it. All figures are scanned drawings; the full figures are on file.

| Figure | Title | Source file | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | County Map and Vicinity Map | Figure_County_Map_and_Vicinity_Map.pdf | Location of the Project in northern Humboldt County, Nevada, relative to Orovada, Winnemucca and State Route 293. |
| 2 | Plan of Operations Boundary and Land Status | Figure_Boundary_and_Land_Status_Figure.pdf | The 10,468-acre Plan of Operations (POO) boundary and land status; all lands within the Project area are public lands administered by the BLM. |
| 3 | Existing Disturbance | Figure_Existing_Disturbance.pdf | Existing disturbance within the Project area, including access roads that existed before January 1, 1981. |
| 4 | Surface Water Bodies and Springs in the Project Area | Figure_Surface_Water_Bodies_and_Springs_Map.pdf | Ephemeral drainages, seasonal springs and streams in and around the Project area, and the watershed divide between the Kings River Valley and Quinn River Valley hydrographic basins. |
| 5 | Geology of the Thacker Pass Project | Figure_Geology_Map.pdf | Geologic map of the deposit area along the western and southern McDermitt Caldera, including the lithium-bearing units and Silica Hill. |
| 6 | Overall Site Layout and Plan of Operations Boundary | Figure_Overall_Site_Layout_and_PoO_Boundary.pdf | Layout of all proposed facilities within the POO boundary, including the three growth media stockpiles. |
| 7 | Proposed Project Facilities | Figure_Proposed_Project_Facilities_Map.pdf | Proposed facilities, the three site entrances from SR 293, haul and secondary roads, and the mine development areas for Phase 1 and Phase 2. |
| 8 | Mine Pit Plan and Cross-Sections | Figure_Mine_Pit_Plan_and_Cross_Section.pdf | Plan view and cross-section(s) of the open pit (North Pit and South Pit), including the proposed explosives storage areas. |
| 9 | Backfill Plan and Cross-Sections | Figure_Backfill_Plan_and_Cross_Section.pdf | Open pit backfill plan and partial backfill cross-sections. |
| 10 | Waste Rock Storage Facility Plan and Cross-Sections | Figure_Waste_Rock_Storage_Facility_Plan_and_Cross_Sections.pdf | Plan and typical cross-section of the West and East WRSFs, including stormwater diversion channels at the toe of the facilities. |
| 11 | Mine Facilities, ROM, and Attrition Scrubbers Plan | Figure_Mine_Facilities_ROM_Attrition_Scrubbers_Plan.pdf | Mine facilities south of the pit, the run-of-mine (ROM) stockpile, and the ore reclaim and attrition scrubbing area. |
| 12 | Coarse Gangue Stockpile Plan and Cross-Section | Figure_Coarse_Gangue_Stockpile_Plan_and_Cross_Sections.pdf | Plan and cross-section of the coarse gangue stockpile (maximum height about 200 feet, 4H:1V slopes). |
| 13 | Lithium Process Plant Layout | Figure_Lithium_Process_Plant_Layout.pdf | Layout of the process plant site, including the sulfuric acid plant, the switchyard and substation, and the connection to the existing Harney Electric transmission line. |
| 14 | Block Flow Diagram | Figure_Block_Flow_Diagram.pdf | Process block flow diagram from acid leaching and neutralization through lithium carbonate, lithium hydroxide and other products. |
| 15 | Clay Tailings Filter Stack and Cross-Sections | Figure_Clay_Tailings_Filter_Stack_Plan_and_Cross_Sections.pdf | Plan and cross-section of the lined clay tailings filter stack (CTFS) east of the process plant. |
| 16 | Stormwater Management Plan and Typical Cross-Section | Figure_Stormwater_Management_Plan_and_Typical_Cross_Section.pdf | Stormwater diversions, sediment ponds and culverts at road crossings, with a typical diversion cross-section. |
| 17 | Water Resources Monitoring Sites | Figure_Water_Resources_Monitoring_Sites.pdf | Surface water and groundwater monitoring sites, including the PH-1 well within the mine pit footprint. |
| 18 | Post-Reclamation Topography | Figure_Post_Reclamation_Topography.pdf | Site-wide post-reclamation contours and topography, and the post-mining configuration of Project facilities. |
| 19 | Clay Tailings Filter Stack Closure Plan | Figure_Clay_Tailings_Filter_Stack_Closure_Plan.pdf | CTFS closure cover (a compacted clay cap overlain by cover soil) and cross-sections of a typical E-Cell. |
